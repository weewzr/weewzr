## Overview

Grant A Wish XXIV is a community engagement initiative that I lead as Project Director. It runs from August 2026 to February 2027 in four phases. As of 15 November 2026 the first phase, Wish Collection, is complete.

This page says little about the people the project serves, and that is on purpose. The beneficiaries are children, so no names, ages, wishes or stories appear here, and the photographs show the volunteer team only.

## Objectives

Grant A Wish is a long-running project of the NUS Students' Community Service Club. An NUS News article on the 2016 edition describes how it works: donors choose and give a gift for a beneficiary themselves, in place of a cash donation. The article describes an earlier edition, so I cite it as background only.

The objectives specific to this edition will be added here together with the details of Phases 2 to 4.

## My role as Project Director

As Project Director I am responsible for the project from start to finish. The role covers planning the four phases, coordinating the volunteers and working with the partner organisations that host the sessions.

## Progress
